# Support

The fastest way to get help is a GitHub issue: [open one here](https://github.com/harrypakhale98/Splt/issues/new/choose). Say what you did, what you expected and what happened. Leave out names and amounts you would rather keep private. The version number is in Settings, under About.

To report a security problem, please do not use an issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/harrypakhale98/Splt/blob/main/SECURITY.md) instead.

## Questions

### Is Splt really free?

Yes. There are no ads, no limits and no subscription, and that will not change. The code is open source, so you can check.

### Do I need an account?

No. Your Apple Account is your identity and there is nothing to sign up for. You can use Splt without iCloud, but then groups stay on your device and cannot be shared.

### Do my friends need Splt?

To share a group and see the same data, yes, and they need iCloud. You can also add someone by name, and they appear in the group as a placeholder. If they join later, you can claim the placeholder so their history carries over.

### Where is my data?

On your device and in your own iCloud. There is no Splt server, and the developer cannot see your groups. Read the [privacy page](privacy) for the details.

### Why is sync not working?

Open Settings in Splt to see the sync status. Check that you are signed in to iCloud on your device and that you have free iCloud storage. Splt keeps changes on the device and uploads them when it can. Sync does not run in the iOS Simulator.

### Can I move my data out?

Yes. Open a group, then Group Settings, and export it as a spreadsheet (CSV) or PDF.
