Onboarding

Three pages that hand something forward: your name becomes your avatar, your avatar's color becomes your segment of the bar, and your currency is the one you split in. It stands out by letting people use Splt's signature control in the first 20 seconds. Every movement is one the app already has, and each one happens because the person did something. Type a name, change the currency, and drag the bar. The three phones at the bottom share the same state, in dark mode.

All three pages, dark

These share state with the walkthrough, so anything you type or drag above shows here too, and the other way round.

What the team decided

Agreed between the design, engineering and skeptic reviews, checked against docs/design.md.

"Sam's share" in ink, not "Sam owes you" in greenGreen means real money coming to you (rule 1), and this money is pretend. The subtitle says "Say you paid…" so it reads as a story, not a balance.
The bar is the real barIt starts equal and the first drag switches it to percentages, like the expense form. It moves in 1% steps, snaps at equal and every 5% with the detent haptic, and keeps a 3% minimum. VoiceOver steps by one percent.
"You" on the bar, not your nameEvery bar in the app says "You" for you. Your name reaches page 3 through your color.
Buttons change pages, and Back is newSwiping between pages would fight the bar's drag. Back also restores the only way back to the Paste card.
Ends on your groups"Start Splitting" and Skip both land on Groups, whose empty state already offers Create Group. No sheet opens by itself, so the first screen is still your groups.
Nothing is saved, nothing celebratesThe demo writes no expense, and it doesn't use up the form's "Drag the dividers" hint. Settling up stays the one celebration (rule 7).
The avatar replaces the strip56pt, your fixed hue (pine). Its initials cross-fade as you type, using Motion.quick. The name field isn't focused for you, because a keyboard rising by itself would cover Continue on an SE.
Fits everyoneWith VoiceOver or accessibility text sizes, page 3 shows the share list instead of the bar, as the form does. Right-to-left languages mirror the bar. With Reduce Motion, page changes become fades.
