Spending, balance links and cover photos, as one set

One direction, drawn in full. Every screen is shown in light and dark side by side. The tokens are the app's own: Ink paper, sheet, text, secondary, tertiary and hairline, owed and owe, the PersonPalette discs, the serif figures, 24pt cards, 14pt controls and 8pt photos.

1 · Photos fill existing shapesIn a list or a group, a photo takes the symbol tile's place (32pt, 8pt corners). Nothing larger. No photo means today's look.
2 · One chart languageThe existing group bar chart plus one new month-bars component, all with the same 2pt bars and no baseline, in shades of ink. Both screens cover the last 12 months and lead with "Your share".
3 · Money looks the sameSerif figures everywhere, with owe and owed colors always paired with words.
4 · People look the sameTheir own color disc, on the web page too.
5 · The web page is SpltPaper, cards, discs, figures, type and dark mode, all from the app.
